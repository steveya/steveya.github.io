\begin{abstract}
This draft will present a discrete Gaussian framework for an exponentially weighted mean-reversion strategy. The central object is an estimated fair-value signal constructed from price history, which converts strategy P\&L into a Gaussian quadratic form. The paper will use this representation to derive expected gross P\&L, distributional shape, turnover, transaction-cost viability, optimal estimator timescales, and calibration penalties.
\end{abstract}
