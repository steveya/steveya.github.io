It is natural to combine our work on Signature Method and Volatility Forecasting. The former is a way to encode path information, and the latter allows us to evaluate features to forecast volatility, albeit in a highly restrictive way. At the time of writing, our work on extending STES to modern machine learning framework such as RNN and GRU is still work in progress, but we can still evaluate signature-based features in simple STES and XGBSTES setups.
