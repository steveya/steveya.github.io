# Short Rate Models Workflow

## Purpose

Use this area for shared instructions governing the full short-rate models series.

## Scope

This file is the local runbook for series-level work on `short-rate-models-*`.

## What Counts as Progress

- richer rate dynamics
- stronger calibration and simulation rigor
- clearer pricing and term-structure implications

## Evidence Expectations

- Keep model assumptions explicit.
- Show calibration and simulation evidence rather than relying on narrative claims alone.
- Tie the math back to pricing or term-structure consequences.

## Workflow Expectations

- Treat the series as one workflow, not as isolated posts.
- Locate the current frontier before editing.
- Update `codex-workflows/short-rate-models-log.md` after a substantive branch.
- Keep new work clearly linked to the prior model in the ladder.

## Deliverables

- Updated narrative or draft notebook for the series
- Clear statement of the new model or calibration step
- Updated `codex-workflows/short-rate-models-log.md`
