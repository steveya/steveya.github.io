# Signature Methods Workflow

## Purpose

Use this area for shared instructions governing the full signature-method series.

## Scope

This file is the local runbook for series-level work on `signature-method-*`.

## What Counts as Progress

- better mathematical grounding
- better augmentation choice
- better empirical usefulness in downstream modeling

## Evidence Expectations

- Keep definitions and notation precise.
- Explain why an augmentation or representation choice is warranted.
- Include empirical evidence or clear downstream relevance when making practical claims.

## Workflow Expectations

- Treat the series as one workflow, not as isolated posts.
- Locate the current frontier before editing.
- Update `codex-workflows/signature-method-log.md` after a substantive branch.
- Keep theory and empirical motivation tied together rather than drifting into separate narratives.

## Deliverables

- Updated narrative or draft notebook for the series
- Clear statement of the mathematical or empirical gain
- Updated `codex-workflows/signature-method-log.md`
